his project introduces an intelligent, off-grid street lighting system powered entirely by renewable solar energy. Developed to address energy conservation needs and reduce reliance on the national power grid, the system utilizes Internet of Things (IoT) technologies for efficient, autonomous operation. It features a decentralized, motion-activated control logic utilizing PIR and LDR sensors, ensuring that the direct current (DC) LED lamps are only fully active when pedestrians or vehicles are detected. Furthermore, the system integrates a local web server to host a centralized dashboard, allowing for real-time remote monitoring, data collection, and fault detection.
